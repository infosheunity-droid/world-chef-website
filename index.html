Order & Delivery Info

Advance Notice Required

To ensure the highest quality and freshest ingredients, all catering orders must be placed at least 48–72 hours in advance. Larger events and custom catering packages may require up to 1 week’s notice.

Order Confirmation

A deposit may be required to secure your booking. Orders are confirmed only after payment arrangements have been completed.

Delivery Service

We proudly offer delivery for catering orders. Delivery fees may vary depending on your location and the size of your order. Delivery times will be scheduled and confirmed upon booking.

Pickup Option

Customers also have the option to pick up their orders at the agreed-upon time and location.

Changes & Cancellations

Any changes to your order should be made at least 24 hours before the scheduled delivery or pickup time. Cancellation policies may apply to confirmed bookings.
