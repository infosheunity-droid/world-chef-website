Policies & Guidelines

Order Policy

  • Orders must be placed at least 24 hours in advance for regular meals.
  • Catering orders for parties, weddings, and events should be booked 7–30 days in advance.
  • All orders are subject to availability.
  • Customers will receive an order confirmation via phone, email, or SMS.
